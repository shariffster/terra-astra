## Delivered scope

Sixteen shipping connections and fourteen cable connections add distinct endpoint pairs among Tokyo, Sendai, Nagoya, Osaka, Fukuoka, Busan, Naha, Taipei, Wenzhou, Fuzhou, Shenzhen, Shantou, Kaohsiung and Manila. These are authored illustrations, not assertions of operating services or surveyed cables. All endpoints are existing Natural Earth-derived offshore anchors. The new downloadable dataset and bundled runtime module share the same provenance and content.

The complete network now contains 518 sea paths and 424 cable paths. Traveller counts, defaults, distribution choices, motion controls, materials, colours, saved settings and the full opening/awakening are unchanged. More geographic connections do not imply more travellers.

Fifteen display spines refine selected incumbent Japanese coastal, Korean, East China Sea and Taiwan approaches. Long ocean crossings meet shared coastal shoulders, while local feeders peel away at several distinct places. The added sea connections share 32 source legs and the cable connections 30; not every local pair is duplicated across both families. Selected Korean, East China Sea and eastern Taiwan hub curves use longer water-constrained approach shoulders. The Nagoya–Osaka feeder was tightened during the bounded visual correction to avoid an unnecessarily wide coastal detour.

Original source files/route records and endpoints are unchanged. Display refinements retain conservative fallback against the existing coarse ETOPO water mask, with no new passage exceptions. Across the complete network, 409 of 415 proposed display refinements pass that mask; six retain their prior source geometry.
