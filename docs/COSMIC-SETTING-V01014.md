## Experience

Earth now sits within a sparse, seeded field of small, steady pearl and warmer stars. Three distant shells contain 4,420 points in total, with only a fraction visible from any camera. Their compact cores and restrained halos sit below Earth's brighter white-gold material. No galaxy band, broad nebula or new route colour family is added. This is an imagined setting, not a mapped astronomical sky; About states that distinction.

“Distant starlight” independently controls the setting from zero to 1.5×. It is included in local saves and exports. Earlier version-one settings remain valid: the missing sky value receives 0.7× while their other values remain intact. The new Balanced defaults modestly lift population and footprint contributions to 0.55× and 0.38×; existing saved values are not silently replaced.

Quiet, Balanced and Rich provide light/density starting points. They preserve the selected lens, transport switches and motion preference. Hold to compare light restores the lighting present when this panel opened, then restores the current settings on release. It compares lighting and density, not a previous lens or camera. It does not write the preview into saved preferences. Pointer cancellation, blur, visibility changes and closing the panel end comparison. The footer distinguishes a composition matching a named save from current changes; last-view automatic persistence remains separate.

The globe moves towards centre when the introductory narrative leaves. Opening controls reserves space beside them, and idle camera orbit pauses while adjustments are made; travellers continue according to the motion preference. Authored city, personal constellation and mobile framing retain their existing arrangements. A collapsed Ask Astra dock now leaves the right-hand control rail reachable on narrow screens.
