## Limits and preservation

The relief mask is coarse, offshore anchors are schematic, and independent crossings remain. This is selected geographic refinement; it does not establish global route accuracy or fully resolve every network-like junction. More quiet connections share the existing traveller pool, so a new route need not carry an animated particle at a given moment. Mobile evidence is a browser viewport, not a physical-device test; static captures do not establish sustained performance, audio quality or owner acceptance. Opening, awakening and reduced-motion logic were not modified.

Disk pressure interrupted one screenshot write. Only three reproducible local deployment archives already saved in native Sites were removed; source, screenshots, tags and native version history were preserved. Final captures succeeded afterward.
