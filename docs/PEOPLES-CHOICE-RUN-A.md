# People's Choice v0.10.0 — Run A

13 September 2026. Lab-only implementation on `people-choice/v0.10`; canonical and the immutable v0.9 archive remain controls. Their exact identities, frozen source and rollback versions are in `PEOPLES-CHOICE-VERSIONING.md`. Native Sites save/deployment records are authoritative for publication; this source document records the candidate and validation.

## First-time journey

Both canonical v0.9 and the initial Lab were inspected at desktop and 390×844 before changes. Genesis was strong. The greatest friction was an unexplained Ask Astra dock, no immediately suggested journey, a utility-style destination list, phone headline/dock collisions, and an abrupt change from accurate city roads to sparse surroundings. Scale names existed but their controls were small. Makkah's existing disclosure was retained as a hard boundary.

Run A keeps the birth of Earth and its 10.8-second choreography. After Earth settles, controls arrive in stages and Astra offers “Show me the vibe in New York.” A visitor can tap once without authentication, or expand Ask Astra for four bounded prompts and a visible Talk action. Navigation yields the panel back to the world. Voice sign-in explicitly leaves exploration available.

“Explore the world” now offers five compact editorial destinations and one short reason to enter each. The chooser states “Mapped detail. Interpretive surroundings & motion.” New York, Palm and Makkah express road networks, designed coastal geometry and collective circulation through their existing destination renderers. No destination, provider or framework was added.

## Continuity and movement

- Band A retains the accurate existing road core.
- Band B selects peripheral mapped roads and creates lower-density interpretive continuation along outward tangents.
- Band C uses sparse, irregular urban hubs and faint filaments, clipped against existing land and deep-water information. Full segments are sampled for water crossings; an East River regression covers the NY gap.
- Luminance and detail fade progressively with radius and scale. The same bounded system serves NY/SG and Palm/Makkah; it is not claimed to be a newly surveyed outer-city road map.
- NY/SG retain 16,000 desktop / 12,000 narrow-screen continuation points across B+C. Palm/Makkah each use 6,000 bounded continuation points. Two band meshes replace the previous single context; one renderer/camera owner remains.
- Makkah uses continuous radii, varied counter-clockwise speeds and sparse arrival/departure paths. The Kaaba anchor is unchanged. No person icons, live tracking, gamification or new data provider.

## Validation

Passed TypeScript, production build, diff whitespace checks, focused UI lint, Live navigation/controller checks, hosted API checks, city continuation, special destinations, world lifecycle, renderer response and Genesis regressions. The lifecycle harness covers context loss/restart, resize, queued commands and cached same-target transitions. Its inert-canvas WebGL messages are expected harness output; they do not substitute for rendered verification.

Native Mac browser inspection covered fresh Genesis and settled Earth; one-click New York; NY City/Street/Region and zoom-out continuity; Singapore; Palm; Makkah City/Street and disclosure; Challenger Deep; return to orbit; desktop and narrow-screen controls. Final responsive confirmation includes the editorial chooser, expanded Astra and the distinct Singapore story layout. Phone widths are simulated on the Mac, not physical-device certification.

During a rendered desktop NY session, the existing diagnostic reported roughly 120 fps / 8.3 ms frames at 1380×989. Existing warm geometry caching and the 1,100 ms same-target scale path remain. This is an observed Mac session, not a cold-network or phone benchmark. The inherited production bundle size warning remains.

Authenticated spoken Live navigation is conditional on an available session. Local status was unconfigured and unsigned-in; catalogue commands were exercised through the same Live-compatible navigation path. Actual microphone/audio validation must be reported separately from these route checks.

## Scope and next decisions

Deliberately cut: additional destinations, broad OSM ingestion, arbitrary-place generation, new providers, live earthquakes, replacements for deterministic aircraft/ships/satellites, model-library experiments, branding changes and canonical crossover. Genesis architecture and stellar systems remain intact; refinement focused on the transition into the usable world.

Run B candidates, at most three: observe a few unaided first-time visitors; test actual iOS/Android performance and authenticated voice; selectively improve outer-city geography only where visitor evidence shows a remaining boundary. More features are not the success criterion.
