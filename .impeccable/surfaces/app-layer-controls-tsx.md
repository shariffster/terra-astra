# v0.10.12 — Calm Earth and layers

**Mode: Experience.** Extend the existing full-screen Earth without changing its identity. The user should perceive luminous geography first, quieter connecting pathways second, and passing travellers as brief accents. Use the existing warm white, sea green, lilac and icy satellite families. No per-route rainbow, explicit political-border layer or satellite orbit mesh.

The memorable moment is a smooth passing light following an almost weightless path while geography remains legible through density. Constant air-corridor clearance removes terrain-driven jaggedness. Rounded water-constrained paths and shared hub tangents preserve smoothness at bends and branch gatherings. Heads and trails use their respective lane geometry. Keep the established scale, Genesis, pause and optional sound systems coherent.

The nonmodal Layers panel belongs to the existing right view rail. Its opaque dark surface, thin border, Georgia heading and compact Arial controls follow the incumbent UI. Explicit close, Escape/focus return, labelled switches, a shared focus radio group, visible keyboard focus and viewport-bounded scrolling make the controls usable on desktop and phone-sized layouts. Family choices are preserved when temporarily hidden by a thematic focus. Separate pathways from travellers without exposing implementation controls.

Night lights brings forward the sourced NASA 2016 field and fades interpretive region markers. Population and human-footprint fields are not implied by brightness and are not implemented. Timing is a deliberate part of the experience: brief quiet fades, geographically staggered returns within seconds, interruptible from current opacity, immediate reduced-motion endpoints. Satellites remain free of visible tracks.

`DESIGN.md` and historical surface briefs remain unchanged context. The v0.10.11 engine brief describes its own checkpoint; its prior review is not a review of v0.10.12. This scope was iterated and visually reviewed by the implementing agent using actual local Chromium captures and a motion recording, including Horizon/Cutaway, Taipei and phone-sized views. No independent review, physical-device or subjective-listening acceptance is claimed. Evidence and remaining limits are in `docs/CALM-EARTH-V01012.md` and the release handoff.
