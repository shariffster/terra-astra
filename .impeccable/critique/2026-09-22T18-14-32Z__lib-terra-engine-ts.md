## Overall judgment

Flow has improved more than perceived richness. The longer Japan/Hawaii approaches feel calmer, and luminous convergence is appropriate. Some fine strands now compress into a single ribbon; the northern crossing still reads like a uniformly parallel band. Preserve the broad geographic triangle and the present celestial setting.

The experience remains specifically Terra Astra: white/gold geography leads, cyan satellites remain readable, and the restrained Moon/nebula give the black sky depth. No additional decorative layers are needed for this pass.

## Two priority issues

1. **P2 — Network hierarchy across viewing scales.** Fine Pacific strands disappear together or accumulate into narrow ribbons, especially at phone scale. Keep strong common trunks, retain a legible middle layer of existing feeder branches, and vary companion visibility gently along the crossing. Adjust by projected size so desktop separation does not vanish into a uniform phone band. Preserve the current route and traveller populations. Suggested work: targeted visual hierarchy and scale adaptation, not another blanket geometry pass.
2. **P2 — Responsive framing around Earth.** In the parent's fresh live 1095 × 998 Pacific view, the italic opening title crosses a busy illuminated rim; the right control rail sits close to the opposite rim. The 390 × 844 saved phone image also shows rail proximity. Reserve clear opening-copy space at narrow desktop widths and a small globe/rail gutter on phones; let the globe reclaim available space during exploration. Wider live 1280 × 720 assessment looked well separated, so this is breakpoint-specific rather than universal.
