## Product Purpose

Terra Astra is an interactive geographic and personal-constellation experience. Visitors explore Earth, adjust its light and illustrated activity, and preserve compositions through browser-local saves or transferable settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Geographic sources and historical observations remain distinct from interpretive light. Marine routes, hub weighting and ocean-current traces are illustrative; they are not live traffic, AIS tracks, surveyed cable alignments or navigation guidance. Preserve source attribution, original route identities and geographic endpoints.

Saved compositions contain light, presentation and layer settings. Browser-local storage is not an account-based service; export/import carries settings between browsers.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Terra Astra's name, constellated Earth and established pearl-and-gold emphasis. Earth remains the focal object; its controls support exploration.
